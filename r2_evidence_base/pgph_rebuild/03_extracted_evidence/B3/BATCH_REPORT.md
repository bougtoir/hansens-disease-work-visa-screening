# B3 evidence report

Northern Africa and Western Asia. The batch covers the assigned jurisdictions in the frozen review frame.

## Search and source inventory

Executed 140 logged searches across 24 jurisdictions. 24 P1, 55 P2, 12 P3, 49 P4. All jurisdictions have an English pass and at least one local-language pass. The register contains 119 saved retrievals, of which 79 were reviewed as policy evidence, case evidence or contextual leads. The other records preserve supplementary, partial, off-topic or unsuccessful download responses. The extraction contains 43 pathways and 172 evidence rows.

The raw query responses and `fetch_*.json` document the search and acquisition trail. The source register lists the opened documents and records URL, UTC retrieval time, bytes, SHA-256 and redistribution status. Selected government pages were captured as rendered text. Partial renderings and portal shells are identified in the register. Local queries and translations were model-assisted and the original wording was preserved. Exact active time per jurisdiction could not be recovered from the parallel search and extraction workflow. Selective follow-ups are recorded in the jurisdiction notes.

UAE government descriptions separately name leprosy in the destination residence programme. They describe unfitness on a positive result at first issue and renewal. The 2021 Wafid regulations were read as version history. The Gulf Health Council reports a subsequent update and its official Arabic FAQ retains leprosy in the unfitness list.

Pathway-level documented absences are PW-CYP-P2-RENEW, PW-GEO-P1, PW-ISR-P1-JPN, PW-LBN-P2, PW-TUN-P2, PW-TUR-P1. Each is limited to its named procedure. ## Key adjudications

- Armenia's official residence law cross-references a disease list. The relevant official list was read separately. Azerbaijan's permit and residence pages explicitly name conditions; the full referenced Cabinet list remains unresolved.
- Algeria's residence form requires general medicine, phtisiology and serology certificates. These labels leave the clinical and serological criteria unspecified. Egypt’s undated Alexandria work-permit procedure specifies an AIDS-free certificate; continuity under Decision 279/2025 remains unresolved.
- Iraq documents an AIDS-related federal entry condition and a separate Kurdistan Region scheme with a country-dependent panel. Jordan’s Ministry of Health certificate workflow specifies named laboratory tests and conditional TB investigation.
- Bahrain and Oman publish destination examination material distinct from Wafid. Oman’s follow-up and conditional fitness provisions concern named conditions other than HD. Qatar and Saudi Arabia provide official evidence of domestic examinations; their full domestic panels remain unresolved.
- Syria’s published consular checklist specifies HIV testing for one-year residence requiring labour authority approval. The positive classification is limited to that documented route. Current domestic implementation and the work-permit requirements remain uncertain.

## Treatment status, removals and safeguards

7 pathway records describe HD rules without an explicit distinction by treatment status. No examined policy text established an exclusion after completed MDT, clinical cure or for residual sequelae. The Wafid FAQ leaves the implications of the unqualified leprosy listing unresolved. The UAE summaries give no definition of a positive result as applied to a previously treated applicant. The 2021 Wafid complaint process concerned the examination service. Current applicant appeal or waiver against HD-related unfitness was unverified.

The case register records OD-KWT-001, OD-KWT-002. Lambert and Walker, `Leprosy Review` 86(1), 108–111 (2015), reports the two removals from Kuwait. Both workers had received treatment or had medication dispensed before removal and completed MDT in Ethiopia later. One had a bacterial index of zero. Clinical non-infectiousness at removal and a completed-MDT exclusion were unverified.

## Reproduction

From the `pgph_rebuild` directory run the batch assembly, report generator and validator.

```sh
python3 03_extracted_evidence/B3/.assemble.py
python3 03_extracted_evidence/B3/.report.py
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 scripts/validate_batch.py B3
```

The validator checks structure and snapshot integrity. Independent verification of the substantive classifications is a separate review step.
