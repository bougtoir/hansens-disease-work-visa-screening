## Method summary

- Engine: Startpage via Chrome/Playwright (Brave/DDG returned no organic results, Mojeek 403); Bing used for a few queries. SERP HTML saved with SHA-256 under `01_raw_search_logs/B8/serp/`.
- Fetch: `requests` (TLS-verify fallback recorded) or Chrome rendering; raw bytes saved under `02_primary_sources/B8/` and never modified; derived text (pdftotext/OCR, tesseract eng/spa/fra) kept separately in `01_raw_search_logs/B8/text/`.
- Stages: P1 English for every jurisdiction; P2 Spanish/French for CRI, CUB, DOM, GTM, HND, HTI, MEX, NIC, PAN, SLV (other jurisdictions have English as official language); P3 targeted statute/regulation queries for JAM, KNA, LCA, TTO, VCT.
- Time budget: ~10-20 min per jurisdiction; not extended. Where a governing instrument could not be retrieved the jurisdiction is left unresolved / cannot_determine.

## Key adjudications

- J-BRB is the only jurisdiction where Hansen's disease is confirmed: Immigration Act Cap.190 prohibits persons with communicable diseases as defined under the health legislation, and the Communicable Diseases (Notification) Regulations 1969 (as amended 2020) list 'Leprosy'. The official medical form items are recorded verbatim in the E-BRB evidence rows; leprosy is not named on the form, and the chest X-ray field was not read as TB-specific beyond the form's own wording.
- Generic 'contagious or infectious disease' clauses (ATG, BHS, BLZ, DMA, GRD, DOM, KNA, SLV) and generic medical/health certificates (DMA, HTI, PAN, VCT, BRB) were not converted into disease-specific findings.
- J-KNA: the Immigration Act cross-refers to regulations under the 'Health Services Act'; the Public Health Act Cap 9.21 retrieved does not contain leprosy wording but is not the referenced instrument, so HD is cannot_determine.
- J-BLZ: 'leprosy asylum' appears only in the domicile/residence-period computation, not as an admission ground, so HD is not_established.
- No observed refusal/denial cases were located; OBSERVED_DENIALS.csv contains header only. Statutory prohibitions were not treated as denials.
- No documented_absence DSS classification was made: no source read states explicitly that no medical requirement exists for the work pathway.
